# Popular UI Design Styles

### 1. Glassmorphism (Glass)
This style mimics the look of frosted glass.
* **Key Features:** Translucent backgrounds (backdrop blur), subtle light borders, vivid background colors.
* **Examples:** [Glassmorphism on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/glassmorphism)

### 2. Minimalism (Mini)
Minimalist UI strips away all unnecessary elements, focusing entirely on functionality.
* **Key Features:** Lots of white/negative space, simple typography, limited color palettes.
* **Examples:** [Minimal UI on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/minimal-ui)

### 3. Neon / Cyberpunk
Inspired by sci-fi, retrowave, and cyberpunk aesthetics.
* **Key Features:** Dark backgrounds, bright glowing neon colors (pink, cyan, purple, lime green), glowing text/borders.
* **Examples:** [Neon UI on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/neon-ui)

### 4. Neumorphism (Soft UI)
Elements look like they are extruded from or pressed into the background material itself.
* **Key Features:** Elements share the exact same color as the background, defined by two shadows (light top-left, dark bottom-right).
* **Examples:** [Neumorphism on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/neumorphism)

### 5. Flat Design
Rejects any illusion of 3D depth.
* **Key Features:** 2D elements, solid and bright colors, sharp edges, simple typography.
* **Examples:** [Flat Design UI on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/flat-design-ui)

### 6. Skeuomorphism
Aims to make digital elements look and function exactly like their real-world physical counterparts.
* **Key Features:** Realistic textures, heavy drop shadows, bevels.
* **Examples:** [Skeuomorphism on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/skeuomorphism)

### 7. Neo-Brutalism (or Brutalism)
A rebellion against "safe" UI, taking inspiration from brutalist architecture.
* **Key Features:** Stark contrasting colors, thick harsh black outlines, hard shadows, bold typography.
* **Examples:** [Neo Brutalism on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/neo-brutalism)

### 8. Aurora (Holographic / Mesh Gradients)
Organic, flowing gradients that look like the Northern Lights.
* **Key Features:** Blurred, multi-color mesh gradients seamlessly blending into one another.
* **Examples:** [Mesh Gradient UI on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/mesh-gradient)

### 9. Claymorphism
A friendly, 3D-like evolution of Neumorphism and Flat Design.
* **Key Features:** Soft clay or playdough look, inner shadows and outer drop shadow.
* **Examples:** [Claymorphism on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/search/claymorphism)

### 10. Material Design
Google's design language using the metaphor of physical paper and ink.
* **Key Features:** Z-axis depth (drop shadows), responsive animations, bold colors.
* **Examples:** [Material Design 3 website](https://m3.material.io/)
